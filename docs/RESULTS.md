## Fixed-noise test

The completed run reports228 rows:12 fixed models × (clean+18AWGN cases), each on158 test records. Model hashes were reported unchanged and clean predictions reproduced. SNR levels are−15,−10,−5,0,5,10dB, with UHF-only, HFCT-only and both-channel corruption.

At both-channel−10dB, GFM accuracy83.54% versus concatenation74.05% (difference9.49 percentage points). At both-channel−5dB, GFM97.47% versus concatenation91.14% (difference6.33 points). These are conditional comparisons, not a claim that GFM wins all cases. At both-channel−15dB, concatenation70.89% exceeds GFM68.35%; at HFCT-only−15dB, equal fusion96.20% exceeds GFM70.89%. Independent HFCT ResNet50 also exceeds GFM in several conditions, including both-channel−10dB.

The user-reported GFM accuracy exceeds the no-confidence variant at all18 noisy settings. Full per-record outputs and image-quality diagnostics are generated by the evaluation scripts and are not included as private data in the repository.
